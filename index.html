Weddings & events

Every ceremony gets its own flowers.

A wedding is five or six events, not one. We plan the flowers for each, so the colours flow from the engagement to the reception and nothing is repeated.

  1. Engagement

    Stage backdrop, ring-tray flowers, entrance and photo corner.

  2. Haldi & mehendi

  3. Sangeet

    Stage and dance-floor décor, hanging florals and table flowers for the night.

    Mandap, pillars, jasmine ceilings, varmala, bridal veni and the petal aisle.

  5. Reception

    Grand stage, flower walls, entrance arch, car décor and centrepieces.

Our work

Maalai, bouquets and the moments they're made for.

Flowers by The Indian Floral at our clients' weddings.

Maalai & garlands

The garland exchange is the heart of a South Indian wedding. We make them fresh on the morning: arugampul (green durva grass), rose, white rose, jasmine and tuberose, plus garlands for elders, guests and the car.

Bouquets

Bridal bouquets, bridesmaid posies and hand-tied gifts, from sunflowers in kraft to blush roses with eucalyptus. Order one from the gift shop.

Bouquets

Picked this morning. In your hands this evening.

Flowers are only as good as the hours since they were cut. We buy early, keep them cool, and tie your bouquet the same day, so it opens at your event, not in our shop.
